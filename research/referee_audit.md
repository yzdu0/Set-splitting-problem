# Internal review

The review found no gap in the selector reduction. It checked:

- One-way selector implications and unique circuit-variable extensions.
- Binomial inversion and polynomial bit lengths.
- Empty, repeated, and contradictory predicates; unused variables.
- General versus fixed clause density.
- The decision kernel, fixed colors, and optimal-coloring recovery.
- The 2-CNF and MAX-SAT extensions.

The paper credits White's coboundary polynomial and keeps the external SAT
counting assumptions explicit. This internal review is not external peer review
and does not verify that theorem or establish publication priority.
